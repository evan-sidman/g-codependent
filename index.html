G-CODEPENDENT tools

A small private helper that the owner of the G-CODEPENDENT YouTube channel uses to publish the channel's own 3D-print timelapse Shorts.

What it does

  • Sets the title, description, audience setting and publish time on videos the channel owner has already uploaded.
  • Reads the channel's own upload list and video analytics to plan future videos.

It only works on the channel of the person who signs in, and only that person runs it, on their own computer. It does not upload, download or delete videos. It is not offered to other users.
